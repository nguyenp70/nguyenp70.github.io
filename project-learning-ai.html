Learning to Harness AI

Published: 06/19/2026

Artificial Intelligence models like Gemini and Claude are incredibly powerful, but their true potential is unlocked through effective prompt engineering. In this project, I dedicated time to deeply understand how to structure prompts, provide optimal context, and guide these models to produce high-quality, reliable outputs for complex tasks.

By experimenting with few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought reasoning, and role-playing directives, I developed a framework for interacting with LLMs that minimizes hallucinations and maximizes alignment with my intended goals. This has fundamentally transformed my approach to software development, automation, and problem-solving.

This page will serve as a living document of my ongoing research into the rapidly evolving field of prompt engineering, showcasing specific techniques, case studies, and code snippets that leverage AI to accelerate productivity.
